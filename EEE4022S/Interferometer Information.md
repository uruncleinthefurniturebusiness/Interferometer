- ***An interferometer is a precise scientific tool that will split a wave into separate paths, recombines them, and studies the resulting interference pattern to measure microscopic changes, distances and surface shapes.***
- Works like so:
	- Splits a wave into separate paths
	- One beam will act as a reference, while the other will travel along a path used for measurement
	- The beams rejoin, which will create light and dark lines (interference fringes) that will reveal the tiny shifts in the waves
- ***Useful for astronomy to combine signals from multiple telescopes to see distant stars with high clarity***
